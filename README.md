# CUE Center for Missing Persons — Modern Corporate Homepage Redesign

A full-width modern corporate concept for **Community United Effort (CUE) Center for Missing Persons** ([ncmissingpersons.org](https://ncmissingpersons.org/)), preserving their foundational **Sector Design** architecture while modernizing the user experience with glassmorphism, responsive mobile-first layouts, and rapid-dispatch emergency features.

---

## 🌟 Overview & Key Features

* **Preserved & Elevated "Sector Design"**:
  * **Regional Search Sectors**: Interactive directory and coverage maps for *South Sector*, *West Coast Sector*, *Midwest Sector*, *Northeast Sector*, *International*, *Unidentified Doe*, and *Unsolved Homicides*.
  * **Signature Program Sectors**: The classic 6 operational pillars (*National Conference, On The Road To Remember Tour, State Outreach Coordinators, HRD Specialty K9 Seminar, Search Resources & Units, Community Awards & Heroes*) reimagined into modern frosted-glass cards.
* **Modern Corporate Glassmorphism (Glass Effect)**:
  * Multi-layer frosted glass panels (`backdrop-filter: blur`), subtle luminous borders, ambient lighting orbs, and high contrast typography.
* **24/7 Crisis Hotline Bar**:
  * Persistent, prominent emergency header with direct click-to-call links for `(910) 232-1687` and `(910) 399-6137`.
* **Interactive Mission Features**:
  * **Confidential Tip Form Modal**: Instant 1-click anonymous tip reporting with case lookup.
  * **Case Intake Form Modal**: Rapid deployment questionnaire for families and law enforcement.
  * **Filterable Case Registry**: Dynamic search bar querying cases by name, sector, and state.
  * **Micro-Donation Engine**: Preset donation tiers ($25, $50, $100, $250, $500) explaining direct mission impact.
* **Official Branding & Real Assets**:
  * CUE Center official logo, 25th Anniversary badge, GreatNonprofits 2024 Top-Rated badge, certified K9 search units, and sponsor emblems.
